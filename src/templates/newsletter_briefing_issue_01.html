“What Happens When Educators Experience AI — Live?”

A glimpse inside the latest AI Passport™ LIVE session by Ekaakshar Education.

{{salutation}}

On 2 October, educators joined us for a live exploration of how emerging AI tools can move beyond conversation and become practical tools for teaching and learning.

Instead of a conventional presentation, the session focused on seeing the workflows happen in real time.

AI PASSPORT™ • OFFICIAL PARTICIPATION CREDENTIAL
FROM THE SESSION
01 • FROM TEXTBOOK TO LESSON PLAN

A Class 10 English chapter was taken from source material through lesson planning, learning outcomes, classroom activities and assessment.

02 • FROM LESSON PLAN TO LEARNING RESOURCES

The workflow extended into visual and multi-format classroom resources designed to support different ways of learning.

03 • FROM MATHEMATICS TO STORYTELLING

A Class 10 Mathematics chapter on Circles was transformed into a bilingual illustrated learning experience.

04 • EDUCATORS AS LEARNING FACILITATORS

The larger conversation moved beyond tools: how technology can help teachers create richer learning experiences while keeping teacher judgement at the centre.

PARTICIPANT FEEDBACK SCORE
9.4 / 10

Average participant rating across school and higher education educators.

“AI supports teachers. Teacher judgement remains central. AI expands what teachers can create, adapt and deliver.”
WHY WE’RE SHARING THIS

We believe school leaders should be able to see what AI-enabled learning actually looks like in practice, rather than simply hearing about AI in education.

AI Passport™ LIVE is our ongoing space for exploring that question with educators.

This edition is simply a glimpse into that work.
