REDEFINING COMPETITION

NOT ANOTHER OLYMPIAD.

AI is changing how we learn, create, solve problems and work. The next generation will need more than knowledge about artificial intelligence. They will need the ability to understand it, use it, question it, create with it and use it responsibly.

AI OLYMPIAD™ is designed to discover and develop that capability.

APPLIED AI CHALLENGES

THEN THE CHALLENGE BEGINS.

The competition evolves beyond assessment into applied challenges. It rewards thinking and capability — not memorisation of AI terminology.

REASON
Analyse information, evaluate logic, and make better decisions using AI-supported reasoning.
CREATE
Use AI tools constructively to develop ideas, synthesize original content, and design solutions.
SOLVE
Approach meaningful real-world problems using structured problem-solving frameworks.
EVALUATE
Question outputs, identify model limitations, detect halluncinations, and refine results.
RESPONSIBLE
Demonstrate ethical judgement, safety awareness, and responsible AI usage boundaries.
