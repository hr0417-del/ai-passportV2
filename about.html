GOVERNANCE MANDATE

The Council Charter

Four core operational directives governing how AI Passport maintains academic rigor, ethical safety, and true industry alignment.

PILLAR 01

Framework Evolution

Continuously updating the 7-stage AI Capability Framework™ to incorporate breakthrough generative AI, agentic workflows, and LLM database orchestration.

PILLAR 02

Ethical Safeguards

Establishing strict guidelines for prompt security, bias reduction, data privacy, and human-in-the-loop audit protocols across all school programs.

PILLAR 03

Industry Alignment

Bridging classroom learning with enterprise expectations by auditing project requirements against active software development roles.

PILLAR 04

Credential Integrity

Ensuring that every issued AI Passport digital ledger entry represents authentic, independently verified hands-on software builds.
