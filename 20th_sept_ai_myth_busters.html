AI MYTH BUSTERS™
Separating Fear From Reality
AI IS EVOLVING.
SO WHAT SHOULD EDUCATORS BELIEVE?
MYTH 01
“AI WILL REPLACE TEACHERS.”
REALITY
AI can automate parts of teaching work.
PLANNING • CONTENT CREATION • DIFFERENTIATION
ASSESSMENT SUPPORT • ADMINISTRATIVE TASKS
BUT TEACHING IS MORE THAN CONTENT DELIVERY.
AI CAN SUPPORT TEACHING.
IT CANNOT REPLACE THE HUMAN RELATIONSHIP AT THE HEART OF IT.
MYTH 02
“AI KNOWS EVERYTHING.”
REALITY
AI can generate, summarise, reason over information and connect patterns.
But it can also:
HALLUCINATE
MISUNDERSTAND
MISS CONTEXT
KNOWLEDGE IS NOT WISDOM.
VERIFY. CONTEXTUALISE. JUDGE.
MYTH 03
“IF AI SOUNDS CONFIDENT,
IT MUST BE CORRECT.”
REALITY
AI output can be:
INCORRECT • OUTDATED • INCOMPLETE • MISLEADING
AI OUTPUT IS A STARTING POINT — NOT AUTOMATIC TRUTH.
ASK
↓
CHECK
↓
VERIFY
↓
USE
MYTH 04
“YOU NEED TO KNOW CODING
TO USE AI.”
REALITY
AI capability now spans:
PROMPTING • RESEARCH • CONTENT CREATION
ANALYSIS • AUTOMATION • BUILDING
CODING CAN EXPAND WHAT YOU BUILD.
BUT IT ISN'T THE ENTRY POINT.
AI LITERACY COMES BEFORE AI ENGINEERING.
MYTH 05
“AI WILL MAKE STUDENTS
STOP LEARNING.”
REALITY
Students can use AI as a shortcut.
WHAT ARE WE ASKING STUDENTS TO DO WITH AI?
ASK → COPY → SUBMIT
QUESTION → EXPLORE → CHALLENGE → CREATE → EXPLAIN
THE GOAL ISN'T TO KEEP AI OUT OF LEARNING.
IT'S TO TEACH STUDENTS HOW TO THINK WITH IT.
