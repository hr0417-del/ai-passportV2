FEATURED DIRECTIVE • JUNE 2026

What Does It Actually Mean to Be AI-Literate?

Traditional education has long defined digital literacy as operating software: typing documents, formatting spreadsheets, or building basic slideshows. However, the emergence of generative AI and autonomous agents fundamentally alters this paradigm.

True AI literacy is not merely knowing how to type a prompt into a chatbot interface. It requires an understanding of model capabilities, data context, prompt engineering, vector database retrieval, and autonomous tool execution.

Key Takeaway: The goal of modern AI education is to transform learners from passive prompt consumers into active product builders who understand how to deploy AI safely and effectively.
